Step 1 of 5

Upload your budget PDFs

Add one PDF or many, e.g. one per state. You can add more at any time.

Step 2 of 5

Which tables should be searched?

Tick the tables that hold the figures you report. Tables spanning several pages are already joined. In budget books each Major Head (e.g. "Grants-in-aid to State Governments") is its own table. If a unit says assumed, the PDF didn't state it on that page, so check it against the PDF.

Step 3 of 5

What should the rows mention?

A row is picked up when its description, or a heading above it, contains any of these words. For example, health also finds the "Charged" row under "Grants for Health Sector". Press Enter to add a word.

Step 4 of 5

Check the rows found

Untick anything you don't want. Rows marked check may need a second look. Click view to see a row in the PDF.

The small grey line above a description shows the headings the row sits under. Total rows add up the rows above them, and rows marked has breakdown are split into Voted/Charged rows below. Keep one level, or you'll count the same money twice.
